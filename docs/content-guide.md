# Content guide

## Voice

Use warm, direct, internationally understandable English. Prefer useful facts
over luxury claims.

Good:

- “An entire home for up to six guests.”
- “Airport pickup may be available for qualifying longer stays.”
- “Exact travel time depends on traffic and destination.”

Avoid:

- unsupported “best,” “perfect,” or “world-class” claims;
- guaranteed pickup or transportation;
- invented prices, fees, bed sizes, dimensions, or travel times;
- language that implies direct booking or payment; and
- publishing access or address details.

## Source of truth

Update durable property facts in `src/data/property.ts`. Keep changing booking
facts—price, availability, fees, cancellation rules, and arrival details—on
Airbnb.

## Reviews

Do not scrape Airbnb and do not invent testimonials. The site may show the
approved rating, review count, host, host status, general value labels, and a
link to verified Airbnb reviews.

## Location

Juwan’s Place should remain approximately 75–80% of the experience. Lapu-Lapu
City and Mactan content should support the property, not become the main
subject.

## Booking language

Every booking area should reinforce:

> For guest protection, reservations and payments are completed through Airbnb
> only.

When the official listing URL is missing, use the explicit “Airbnb link coming
soon” state. Never add a guessed or fake listing URL.
